## Featured apps

### OpenPaint
A familiar paint app for **Linux and macOS**. Sketch, paint and make quick image edits with brushes, shapes, text and layers. Save editable OpenRaster projects or export your images. Editing works offline, with no account or telemetry.

[**Explore OpenPaint →**](https://github.com/AfshinKI/openpaint) · [Download](https://github.com/AfshinKI/openpaint/releases/latest) · [Installation guide](https://github.com/AfshinKI/openpaint/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md)

### PanelTag
A tiny, customizable name badge for your **Ubuntu GNOME top panel**. Label your machines Work, Home or Lab so you always know which computer you’re using. Choose the name, color, icon, size and position. No account or tracking.

[**Try the PanelTag demo →**](https://afshinki.github.io/PanelTag/) · [Source code & installation](https://github.com/AfshinKI/PanelTag)
